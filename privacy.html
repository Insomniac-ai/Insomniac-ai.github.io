Privacy Policy

Lappy-U rclone is a personal integration used by its owner to connect rclone to Google Drive.

The integration permits rclone to read, create, modify, and delete files in the owner's Google Drive.

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. The integration does not operate an independent server for collecting or selling personal data.

Google Drive files are accessed and transferred by rclone at the owner's direction.
